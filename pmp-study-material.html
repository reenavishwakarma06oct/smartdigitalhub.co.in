PMP Study Plan

A structured study plan can help candidates divide their preparation into manageable sections.

Week 1 — Fundamentals

Review project management fundamentals, project life cycles, teams, stakeholders and key terminology.

Week 2 — Project Management Concepts

Study areas such as scope, schedule, cost, quality, resources, communication, risk and procurement.

Week 3 — Agile & Hybrid

Review Agile principles, Scrum concepts, iterative approaches, prioritization and hybrid project management.

Week 4 — Practice & Revision

Complete original practice questions, review mistakes and revise important concepts.
