Interview preparation

Common interview questions (with quick tips)

Practice these frequently asked interview questions before your next job interview in India. For personalised practice, book a mock interview session.

General & HR Interview Questions

  1. Tell me about yourself.Keep it work-relevant: a short summary of your background, one or two key strengths and why you're interested in this role.
  2. Why do you want to work here?Show you've researched the company and connect your goals to what the role actually involves.
  3. What are your strengths and weaknesses?Pick a real weakness and describe what you're doing to work on it, not a disguised strength.
  4. Why should we hire you?Match your specific skills and experience to the job description, not generic qualities.
  5. Where do you see yourself in five years?Show growth and commitment without sounding rehearsed or unrealistic.

Behavioral Interview Questions

  1. Describe a time you handled a conflict at work.Use a real example: the situation, the action you took and the outcome.
  2. Tell me about a time you missed a deadline or made a mistake.Focus on what you learned and changed afterward, not just the mistake itself.
  3. Describe a time you had to work under pressure.Explain how you prioritised tasks and stayed organised, with a concrete result.
  4. Give an example of when you worked in a team to solve a problem.Highlight your specific contribution, not just the team's outcome.
  5. Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly.Show how you approach unfamiliar situations and what resources you used.

Interview Questions for Freshers

  1. Why did you choose this field or course?Connect your academic choices to your career interest and the role you're applying for.
  2. What projects or internships have you worked on?Describe your role, what you built or learned, and the result — even for academic projects.
  3. How do you handle working without much prior experience?Emphasise your willingness to learn and any transferable skills from college, internships or side projects.
  4. What do you know about our company or industry?A few minutes of research on the company website and recent news goes a long way here.
  5. What are your salary expectations?Give a realistic range based on role and location research rather than a single fixed number.

Career Change Interview Questions

  1. Why are you switching careers or industries?Frame it as a considered decision tied to your goals, not as running away from your last role.
  2. How is your previous experience relevant to this role?Identify transferable skills explicitly — don't assume the interviewer will connect the dots.
  3. Aren't you overqualified or underqualified for this position?Address the concern directly with specific examples of relevant capability.
  4. How will you handle being new to this field?Show a learning plan: courses, certifications or skills you're already building.
  5. Why should we hire someone without direct experience in this industry?Lead with the fresh perspective and proven skills you bring instead.
