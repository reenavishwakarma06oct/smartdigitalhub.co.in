1. Plan 30 Days of Social Media Content

Start by choosing one goal for your business: building awareness, explaining your products, generating enquiries or encouraging repeat purchases. Each post should support one of these goals.

Use four content categories

  • Educational posts that answer customer questions.
  • Product or service posts that explain your offer.
  • Behind-the-scenes posts that show your process.
  • Engagement posts that invite genuine questions and opinions.

Prepare a weekly schedule, write captions in batches and review which posts receive useful engagement. Adjust the next month's plan based on real results rather than guessing.

2. Use ChatGPT for Small Business Marketing

ChatGPT can help you brainstorm post ideas, draft captions, outline campaigns and turn common customer questions into educational content. Give it your target audience, tone, offer and objective for better results.

Review all output before publishing. Check factual claims, remove generic wording and add your own knowledge of the business. Never invent testimonials or customer results.

Example prompt: Create five original social media post ideas for a local bakery. Include a clear purpose, a short caption and a relevant call to action for each post.

3. Start Digital Marketing With a Small Budget

Begin with one service you can deliver reliably. Create a few sample projects, define the deliverables and contact businesses that could genuinely benefit from your work.

  1. Choose a specific customer type.
  2. Create original samples to demonstrate your skills.
  3. Set a clear introductory price and delivery timeline.
  4. Contact suitable prospects with personalised messages.
  5. Track replies, sales, expenses and customer feedback.

Start with free tools and reinvest only after you have evidence that customers are willing to pay.
